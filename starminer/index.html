StarMiner

Short-form Bitcoin explainers, a repeatable production workflow, and public client delivery.

The goal

Explain Bitcoin concepts in short vertical videos and make client review and downloads straightforward.

My contribution

Production workflow, review coordination and client delivery.

The work covers a shared visual system, word-timed captions, animated story events, dual audio versions, technical checks and public delivery.

Delivered

  • 22 vertical episodes with main and no-music versions
  • English subtitles, portrait covers and posting copy
  • One compact review directory and individual watch pages

Scripts and storyboards were supplied; episodes 13–22 are Codex drafts from Claude outlines. The production workflow uses AI narration and code animation. Creator/director publication review is pending. No TikTok performance or audience results have been measured.
