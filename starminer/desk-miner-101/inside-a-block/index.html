What's actually inside a Bitcoin block?

Quick note: StarMiner sent me this miner.

First, a batch of transactions, all bundled together.

Then a header with the fingerprint of the block before it.

One special entry pays the finder: new bitcoin plus the fees.

Seal it with a valid hash, and it joins the chain.

Solo mining does not guarantee rewards.
