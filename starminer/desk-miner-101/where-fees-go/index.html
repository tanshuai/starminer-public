Where do Bitcoin transaction fees go?

Quick note: StarMiner sent me this miner.

When you send bitcoin, you can add a small fee.

The miner who puts your transaction in a block collects that fee.

Together with new bitcoin, those fees make up the block reward.

Every miner tries to be the one that collects it.

Solo mining does not guarantee rewards.
