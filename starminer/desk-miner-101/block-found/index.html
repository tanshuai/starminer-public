What actually happens when a miner finds a block?

Quick note: StarMiner sent me this miner.

First, it sends the new block out to the network.

Computers everywhere check it against the rules.

If it checks out, it's added, and the finder gets new bitcoin plus fees.

Then everyone starts over on the very next block.

Including my little miner.

Solo mining does not guarantee rewards.
