Why is it called a blockchain?

Quick note: StarMiner sent me this miner.

Each block stores the fingerprint of the one before it.

That links the blocks together into a chain.

Change an old block, and every link after it breaks.

That's why Bitcoin's history is so hard to rewrite.

My little miner tries to add the next link.

Solo mining does not guarantee rewards.
